The two-flavour oscillation probability, the starting point for what
long-baseline experiments like NOvA and DUNE measure:

\[
P_{\nu_\mu \to \nu_\tau} = \sin^2(2\theta)\,
\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\Delta m^2 L}{4E}\right)
\]

Lorentz invariance violation adds direction-dependent terms to this picture,
which is what the sidereal-effect searches look for.
